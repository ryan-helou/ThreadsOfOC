# Threads of OC

**A private web app for keeping track of a small streetwear resale business: what's in stock, what sold, and how much profit it made.**

Someone who buys and resells clothing (brands like BAPE, Hellstar and Denim Tears) needs to know which pieces and sizes are still available, who bought what, and whether the business is making money. This app keeps all of that in one place. It has a single owner login, and nobody else can make changes.

## What you can do with it

- Keep a catalogue of items with photos. Each size is tracked on its own, with its cost and whether it's available or sold.
- Add new items quickly by uploading a batch of photos.
- Browse stock as a photo gallery or as a compact grid.
- Record a sale: which item and size, the buyer, the price and how it was handed off.
- See the finances: total profit, profit by month and by brand, top-selling items and top buyers.
- Download a backup of everything as a file and restore it later.
